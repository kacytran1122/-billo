# Billo Red-Flag Screener

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Checks symptoms for emergency signs **before** anyone shops for care. Uses fixed safety rules, not AI guesses. If it finds a danger sign, it says "Call 911" or "Go to the ER now". Part of Billo.

**Keywords:** emergency check, symptom triage, when to go to the ER, chest pain, stroke signs.
