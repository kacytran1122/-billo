# Billo After-Care Organizer

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Turns a paid receipt or unpaid final bill into a verified care record. It matches the provider, final amount, saved estimate, and visit log; then it routes the case to safe payment, optional benefit checks, or follow-up planning. It never promises reimbursement without a benefit source named by the patient. Part of Billo.

**Keywords:** after-care, medical receipt organizer, care record, benefit eligibility, HSA, FSA, reimbursement documents, follow-up care.
