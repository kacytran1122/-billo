# Billo Care Finder

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Finds urgent care, clinics and ERs near you and shows **real prices** from public hospital price files (CMS price transparency), with one picture of each place. Try: "How much is a knee X-ray near Westwood?" Part of Billo.

**Keywords:** urgent care near me, hospital prices, X-ray cost, cheapest clinic, price transparency, self-pay.
