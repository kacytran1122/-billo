# Billo

**Make every care dollar count.**

Billo is an AI care companion that helps patients understand costs before care and organize bills, receipts, benefits, reimbursements, and next steps afterward. One conversation keeps the care plan, expected price, visit record, and final document connected.

## Why Billo exists

Patients are often asked to choose care before they know the price, then decode unfamiliar bills and benefit rules after the visit. Billo brings those disconnected steps into one guided experience.

## Before care

1. Tell Billo what happened and where you are.
2. Billo checks fixed emergency rules first.
3. It compares nearby care options with clearly labeled estimated-price context.
4. Choose a place and save the expected price.
5. During the visit, tell Billo what services were added to keep a running total.

## After care

1. Upload a final bill or paid receipt.
2. Billo reads and organizes the document.
3. It matches the provider, charges, and total to the saved visit.
4. Name a real HSA, FSA, HRA, insurance, employer, school, travel, or accident benefit to check possible reimbursement paths.
5. Review every result before taking the next step.

## Try these prompts

- "My knee hurts after soccer and I am near UCLA."
- "Book option 1."
- "They did an X-ray."
- "Here is my unpaid final bill."
- "Here is my paid receipt and I have an HSA."
- "Show my case status."

## Safety and privacy

- Emergency indicators stop the shopping flow and direct the patient to urgent help.
- Benefit results are possible paths, not eligibility decisions or guarantees.
- Billo never treats silence as consent.
- Payment and reimbursement demonstrations are simulations. No card data is collected and no money moves.
- Uploaded documents are removed after extraction and monitoring uses a protected case identifier.

## Built for LA Hacks 2026

Billo connects ASI:One and Fetch.ai agents with Photon messaging, TigerData case memory, Sentry monitoring and recovery, and simulated Visa payment and reimbursement safeguards.

Every patient, provider, document, price, and payment shown in the demo is synthetic. Billo does not provide medical, legal, insurance, tax, or financial advice. In an emergency, call 911.
