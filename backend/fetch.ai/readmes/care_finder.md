# Billo Care Finder

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Finds urgent care, clinics, and ERs for the requested location and shows transparent estimated-price context. Demo results are synthetic and clearly labeled; live results must identify their source and are not final provider quotes. Try: "How much is a knee X-ray in Chicago?" Part of Billo.

Care comparison preview: https://www.getbillo.us/tools/prices

The preview uses synthetic data and is not a live quote or a private case record.

**Keywords:** urgent care near me, hospital prices, X-ray cost, cheapest clinic, price transparency, self-pay.
