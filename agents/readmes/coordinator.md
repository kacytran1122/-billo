# Billo · Care without the sticker shock

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Billo helps patients who pay for care themselves. Tell Billo what happened, and it checks for emergency signs, finds affordable care near you with **real hospital prices**, books it, and saves a Good Faith Estimate. When the bill comes, send a photo: Billo checks it **line by line, live**, finds duplicate charges, code errors and prices far above the area, writes the dispute letter, and pays **only the fair amount** with Visa after you approve.

**Try:** "My knee hurts after soccer, I'm paying myself, near UCLA." · "Here is my bill (demo)" · "approve"

**Keywords:** medical bill, hospital bill check, overcharge, billing error, urgent care cost, price transparency, Good Faith Estimate, No Surprises Act, charity care, self-pay, healthcare payment, Visa.

Not medical or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911.
