# Billo · Care without the sticker shock

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Billo helps patients plan affordable care before a visit and organize everything afterward. It checks emergency signs, finds care with real prices, books it, and saves an estimate. After care, it turns a final bill or paid receipt into a verified record, coordinates next steps, and checks possible reimbursement paths only when the patient names a real benefit. Its Visa flows are clearly labeled simulations: no card data is collected and no funds move.

**Try:** "My knee hurts after soccer, I'm paying myself, near UCLA." · "Here is my unpaid final bill (demo)" · "scan clinic QR demo" · "approve clinic payment"

**Keywords:** after-care organizer, medical receipt, HSA, FSA, reimbursement documents, follow-up care, urgent care cost, price transparency, self-pay.

Not medical or legal advice. In an emergency, call 911.
