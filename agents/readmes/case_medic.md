# Billo Case Medic (helper)

![tag:innovationlab](https://img.shields.io/badge/innovationlab-3D8BD3)
![tag:hackathon](https://img.shields.io/badge/hackathon-5F43F1)

Wakes on a Sentry alert, reads the trace, and picks one of three fixed fixes for a Billo case: retry the bill reader, continue with an unverified NPI result, or pause payments. Then it tells the patient in plain words.
